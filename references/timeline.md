# Application timeline

Plugin-authored summary of Article 113 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 as amended by Article 1(40) of Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, plus the transitional rules in Article 111 as amended by Article 1(39). Check the wording in `articles/113.md` and `articles/111.md` before relying on a date. Each opens with the 2026 amending text.

Compiled against the texts published on 12 July 2024 and 24 July 2026. Later amendments are not reflected. If today's date is well after July 2026, check for newer amending acts on EUR-Lex (CELEX 32024R1689, "Current consolidated version" and "Amended by").

## Legacy high-risk systems

High-risk systems placed on the market or put into service before the Chapter III date that applies to them fall under the Regulation only after a significant change in design, except public-authority systems, which have until 2 Aug 2030 (Art. 111(2) as amended). Article 5 prohibitions apply regardless.

## Agent inference, not stated in the text

- Art. 49 registration and Art. 43 conformity assessment sit in Chapter III Section 5, which applies from 2 Aug 2026. They attach to systems that are high-risk under Sections 1 to 3, so in practice they bite when those sections apply to the system. Flag this reading as inference and recommend counsel when a decision depends on it.
- National penalty regimes and competent authorities differ by Member State. The Act sets maxima (Art. 99), not the amounts actually imposed.
