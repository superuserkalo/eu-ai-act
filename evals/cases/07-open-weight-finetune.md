# Open-weight fine-tune

A German research lab fine-tunes a popular open-weight 70B general-purpose language model on legal texts using about 2% of the original pre-training compute, and publishes the resulting weights on Hugging Face under the Apache-2.0 licence with a model card. Anyone can download it. The base model's provider is not in the EU. The lab is a non-profit but the release is not part of a specific scientific research project.
